Connect Google Analytics to see visitors, sources and pages here

The website already sends data to Google Analytics (property {{ measurement_id }}), after visitors accept the cookie banner. Reading it here takes a one-time setup of about ten minutes:

  1. In Google Cloud (console.cloud.google.com) create a project, turn on the Google Analytics Data API, and create a service account with a JSON key.
  2. In Google Analytics open Admin > Property access management and add that service account's email as a Viewer.
  3. Copy the numeric Property ID from Admin > Property details.
  4. On the server set GA4_PROPERTY_ID and either GA4_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_FILE (path to the key) or GA4_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON, then restart.

The full walk-through is in marketing/google-analytics-guide.md. Everything else on this page works without it.

How these numbers are made

Visits are counted by Kauli itself, without cookies: a visitor is a daily-changing hash, so nobody is followed from one day to the next, and no address is kept. People who send Do Not Track are not counted. Sign-ups, customers, orders, payments and activity come from our own records. Purchase likelihood and churn risk are small models over what each customer did (uploaded, translated, looked at plans, came back, went quiet); the likelihood model starts from sensible weights and learns from your customers once there are enough of them.
