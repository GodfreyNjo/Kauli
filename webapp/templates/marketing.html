How it works

Every order follows the same four steps, whether it's a five-minute clip or a full episode.

1

Upload or link a video

Drop an audio/video file, or paste a YouTube link - we pull the audio straight from the source, no re-uploading a file you've already published.

2

AI drafts it in minutes AI

Speech recognition, translation, and (if you've ordered a dub) voice synthesis produce a first pass fast - usually before you've finished your coffee.

3

Human verification on every single word HUMAN

A real editor reviews and corrects the transcript and translation word-by-word before anything is marked ready - AI drafts, people verify.

4

You download, ready to publish

Transcript, subtitles (SRT/VTT), translated text, or a dubbed audio/video track - whichever you ordered, sized to your plan.
