Help & troubleshooting

Common questions about what's happening with your order, your minutes, and your account. If nothing here solves it, email or WhatsApp us directly - a real person reads every message, not a bot.

My order says "Analysis in progress" and hasn't changed - is it stuck?

Almost always not stuck - it just means the file hasn't finished uploading and being probed yet (checking its real length and format before it can be priced). Once that's done you'll see the language/service pills fill in and, if payment is needed, a "Pay now" prompt. If it's been more than a few minutes on a short file, or the page hasn't changed after a refresh, that's worth reaching out about.

My order is "queued" or "processing" - how long should that take?

Every order gets a real, calculated delivery estimate the moment it's confirmed, based on its actual length and the service you picked - you'll see it on the order page as a "time remaining" bar, not a vague promise. Orders update themselves automatically while they're active, so you don't need to keep refreshing.

I paid by bank transfer - why hasn't my order started?

Bank transfer is the one payment method that isn't confirmed automatically - there's no live bank integration, so a real staff member manually confirms once the transfer has actually landed, same as any business without automated bank reconciliation would. Card payments (Paystack) and M-Pesa confirm automatically and start right away. If a bank transfer has been pending for a while, email or WhatsApp us the reference number shown on your payment page and we'll check on it directly.

Why is it asking me to pay when I still have free minutes?

Your free monthly minutes only ever cover transcription-only orders - they're a way to try the AI-drafted, human-checked quality for yourself before spending anything, not a way to get a free full dub or translation. If you specifically picked "Transcription only" and it's still asking for payment, that's worth flagging to us directly - it shouldn't happen.

If you've bought prepaid credits separately (a wallet top-up), those apply automatically to any service level, right after your free minutes, at the same fixed value regardless of which service they're spent on - you don't need to do anything to use them, and you can see your current credit balance on the Billing page.

My order was "returned" without completing - what do I do?

This means our team flagged something they needed from you before continuing - check the message thread on that order's page for the specific reason. Reply there directly, and if we need a corrected file, attach it right on that same page - there's a "Resume this order" button once you have. No need to submit a whole new order or pay again; a returned order just won't pick itself back up until you do.

My order shows "Something went wrong on our end"

This means the processing job failed after a few automatic retries. You don't need to do anything - our team is notified automatically the moment this happens and looks into it. If you're on a real deadline, email or WhatsApp us the order so we can prioritize it.

I can't log in / forgot my password

Use Forgot your password? on the sign-in page - it emails you a real reset link. If you don't receive it within a few minutes, check spam before contacting us.

What does "voice cloning consent" mean, and do I need to check it?

Only if you specifically want the dubbed track to sound like the original speaker's actual voice, rather than one of our standard voices. Leave it unchecked if you're not sure - you can always grant consent later from the order page once it's running, nothing is lost by waiting.
