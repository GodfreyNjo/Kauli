1. What Kauli is

Kauli transcribes, translates and (optionally) dubs audio and video between Swahili, English and Kikuyu. Every AI-drafted transcript and translation is reviewed by a human editor before it's marked ready for delivery. Kikuyu currently uses a human transcriber instead of a speech-recognition model - no production-ready one exists yet, though research versions do - see our blog post on this for the full honest answer.
