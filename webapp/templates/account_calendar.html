Where do I find it in Google Calendar?
  1. Open Google Calendar on a computer, click the gear, then Settings.
  2. On the left, click your calendar under Settings for my calendars.
  3. Scroll to Integrate calendar and copy the Secret address in iCal format.
Where do I find it in Outlook?
  1. Open Outlook on the web, click the gear, then Calendar > Shared calendars.
  2. Under Publish a calendar, choose your calendar and set it to Can view all details, then Publish.
  3. Copy the ICS link.

The link lets Kauli read this calendar's events, nothing else. Treat it like a password: we store it privately, show it masked, and delete it when you disconnect. You can also reset it in your calendar settings at any time.
