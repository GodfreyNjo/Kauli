## Upload steps

1. YouTube Studio, **Create, Upload videos**, choose `kauli-guides-ep01-youtube.mp4`.
2. Paste the title and description. Upload `thumbnail-ep01.jpg` as the custom thumbnail (this needs the channel to be verified: studio.youtube.com, Settings, Channel, Feature eligibility).
3. **Subtitles** step: choose English, **Upload file, With timing**, pick `captions-ep01.en.srt`.
4. Add the chapters automatically: YouTube reads them from the description (the first must be 00:00, and there must be at least three).
5. **Cards** at 04:48, 07:47 and 11:12 pointing to other videos or the playlist once you have them; **end screen** in the last 20 seconds (Subscribe plus a video element).
6. Channel customisation: **Branding** tab, upload `channel-banner.png` and `channel-avatar.png`.
7. Pin a comment: *"Which transcript mistake have you seen the most? Names, numbers or speakers? Tell us and we'll cover it in a future episode."*
