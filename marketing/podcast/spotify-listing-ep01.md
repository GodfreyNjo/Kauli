**Episode description (paste this):**

Your transcript says "Nye Robbie" instead of Nairobi, and "forty clients" became "fourteen". Why does it happen, and how do you stop it?

In this episode, Amara and Dan walk through every way a transcript, subtitle file, translation or dub goes wrong, and the fix a professional would use for each:

- Why AI transcripts fail in plausible-looking ways, and why one accuracy number never tells you about your file
- Fixing it before you hit record: microphone distance, the room, noise, one voice at a time, and why you should upload the original file
- Names, numbers and jargon: word lists, spelling on the recording, and checking every figure against the audio
- Speaker labels, accents, and conversations that switch between languages
- Subtitles people can actually read: line length, timing, where to break a line, and keeping captions clear of text already on screen
- Translating from the corrected transcript, so one fix reaches every language
- Dubbing, matching length, keeping a voice consistent, and why consent matters
- AI or human review, hidden costs to watch for, and three privacy questions to ask any service
- A ten-point cheat sheet you can screenshot
